# Official rule review - 12 November 2026

Source: [official rules](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules), accessed
12 November 2026. This is a technical submission checklist, not an eligibility
determination by the organizers.

The deadline is 12 November 2026 at noon Pacific. Sandbox integration is
explicitly permitted. PayPal must be central alongside an AI tool. Existing
projects are allowed if significantly updated during the submission window;
explain those updates. The rules do not explicitly require payment capture.

Provide a working demo, public licensed repository with complete setup, and a
public YouTube demonstration under three minutes. Supply necessary testing
access in the submission instructions. Keep service secrets out of source.
Access must remain available through judging, which ends 15 December 2026.

The five judging criteria have equal weight: implementation, design, impact,
innovation and presentation. Demonstrate the actual supported flow and describe
remaining limitations consistently across video, site and submission.

The problem is specific: an AI purchasing recommendation must not become
unrestricted spending permission. This application validates catalog choices,
binds purchase terms, prepares real Sandbox orders, and refuses changed terms.
That demonstrates a useful control. Completed approval, buyer consent, capture
and verified receipt remain a stronger payment story and must be proven before
claiming them. A three-product catalog does not establish broad shopping utility
or customer adoption.
